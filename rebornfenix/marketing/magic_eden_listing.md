**Q2: What is the difference between the Founding Warrior and the Legion of Honour?**
Two entirely different NFT types. The Founding Warriors are 22 people who minted at $100 USDC. **Q3: How do I personalize my Founding Warrior NFT?**
During the Founding Warrior mint process, you will be asked to submit your chosen name and your zodiac sign. Earlier arrival means earlier generation. There is no other variable.
