## SERVER RULES (posted in #server-rules)

---

> # THE CODE OF THE HALL
>
> *These are not suggestions. These are the terms of your presence here.*
>
> ---
>
> **I. No tolerance for manipulation.**
> No coordinated pump discussions. No shill campaigns. No false information spread to benefit your position. This results in a permanent ban, no appeal.
>
> **II. Honesty is the only currency.**
> Share real analysis with real reasoning. Share real losses alongside wins. If you're wrong, say so. If you don't know, say so. The warrior who performs confidence they don't have is dangerous to everyone around them.
>
> **III. Respect the difference.**
> This community was built with neurodiverse traders in mind. Different communication styles are welcome. Different risk tolerances are welcome. Different trading philosophies are welcome. Personal attacks on people for being different are not.
>
> **IV. Holders-only spaces are sacred.**
> Do not share content from holder-only channels with non-holders. Do not screenshot private discussions for external sharing. Violation results in immediate ban.
>
> **V. No external project shilling.**
> You can mention other projects in passing. You cannot run a promotional campaign for them in this server. This is REBORNFENIX territory.
>
> **VI. Moderators are warriors too.**
> The @Fenrir Guard are community members who stepped up to serve. They have the final word on rule interpretation. Arguing with a moderation decision in public rather than DM'ing your appeal is itself a rule violation.
>
> **VII. Rise or leave.**
> If you are here only to extract — only to take information without contributing, only to lurk without participating, only to hold an NFT you have no connection to — the community will not miss you when you sell. The doors are open. The hall is for warriors.
>
> ---
>
> *Breaking rules I or IV results in immediate permanent ban.*
> *Breaking rules II, III, V, VI, VII results in warning → 24h mute → permanent ban on third violation.*

## PINNED MESSAGES (key channels)

---

### Pinned: #market-fire

> **HOW TO POST ANALYSIS IN THE MARKET-FIRE CHANNEL**
>
> Great analysis includes:
> ✓ The asset you're watching
> ✓ The timeframe
> ✓ What you see (structure, levels, indicators)
> ✓ What you think it means
> ✓ What would invalidate your thesis
> ✓ What you're doing about it (or not doing and why)
>
> Bad posts:
> ✗ "BTC going up"
> ✗ "Anyone else watching ETH?"
> ✗ "LONG [token] NOW 🚀🚀🚀"
>
> We're warriors, not signal callers. Show your thinking or don't post.

---

### Pinned: #war-stories

> **WAR STORY FORMAT**
>
> *Optional template — feel free to write it your own way*
>
> **The Setup:** What were you doing? What did you think was going to happen?
>
> **The Fall:** What went wrong? How bad did it get? Don't soften it.
>
> **The Moment:** What happened when you hit bottom? What did you do?
>
> **The Rise:** What did you learn? What changed? Where are you now?
>
> There is no shame here. Every story of loss that someone shares honestly is a lesson someone else doesn't have to learn the expensive way.
>
> The only rule: be true. Embellished losses and exaggerated recoveries help no one.

---
