Each rune carried a specific power that the bearer would embody. Warriors did not choose their rune lightly. The rune chose them, in the crucible of trial.

REBORNFENIX is not another PFP project. It is a permanent record on the Solana blockchain — proof that you faced your losses, faced your fear, and chose to keep building. 22,222 warriors. Each carrying a unique rune forged from the fires of real experience. This is the brotherhood of those who refuse to stay down.

REBORNFENIX (RBFX) is a collection on the Solana blockchain with two tiers: 22 Founding Warriors (100 USDC each — personalized imperial purple brasão NFTs) and 22,222 Legion of Honour (10 USDC each — circular medal NFTs organized into 10 generations of 2,222 warriors each). Born from a personal story of financial loss and recovery — a manifesto in NFT form for everyone who has faced defeat and chosen to rise.
There are only 22 Founding Warriors — ever. They receive a fully personalized imperial purple brasão NFT with their chosen name arc, zodiac shield, and a unique rune sword that will never appear on any other NFT. They also receive permanent placement on this website and access to the private Warrior Council channel. The Legion of Honour consists of 22,222 warriors organized into 10 generations, each receiving a circular medal NFT in their generation's colour.
The Legion of Honour mints in sequential order. Tokens #1–2,222 are Generation 1 (Phoenix Guard, red). Tokens #2,223–4,444 are Generation 2 (Gold Battalion, gold). And so on through 10 generations of exactly 2,222 warriors each, ending with Generation 10 (Iron Born, iron grey) at tokens #19,999–22,222. The earlier you mint, the earlier your generation. Your generation is your destiny — it is determined at the moment of mint and never changes.

Your personalized brasão NFT will be hand-configured and delivered to your wallet within 48–72 hours. Only 22 spots exist — once they are gone, they are gone forever.

Click "Join the Legion — 10 USDC", approve the transaction, and your medal NFT will be assigned to your wallet within seconds. Your generation is determined by the current mint count at the moment of your transaction.
REBORNFENIX mints on Solana Mainnet using the Metaplex Token Metadata standard — the same standard used by the largest Solana NFT projects. This means your NFT is compatible with all major Solana wallets (Phantom, Solflare, Backpack) and marketplaces (Magic Eden, Tensor). The mint price is paid in USDC-SPL — no native SOL required beyond a tiny gas fee.
Yes. The REBORNFENIX mint contract is built using Metaplex's Candy Machine v3, the industry-standard minting framework on Solana with hundreds of millions in minted volume. Additional security measures include bot protection via allowlist verification, on-chain randomness for trait assignment, and an immutable metadata freeze post-mint.
